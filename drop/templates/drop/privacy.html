Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

01. What is SpiddyWeb?

SpiddyWeb is a free, anonymous temporary file-sharing service. You upload a file, get a 6-digit PIN, share that PIN with someone — they download the file. That's it. No accounts, no profiles, no history.

02. What data do we collect?

We collect as little as possible:

  • → The file you upload — stored temporarily on our server, automatically deleted after 24 hours.
  • → Upload timestamp — so we know when to delete the file.
  • → CSRF token (session cookie) — a standard Django security mechanism to protect form submissions. It stores no personal information and expires when you close your browser.

We do not collect: your name, email, IP address, device info, location, or any account data.

03. How are files stored?

Uploaded files are stored on the server disk under a randomly named path. They are not encrypted at rest. After 24 hours from upload, the file is permanently deleted from disk and our database. There is no backup or recovery after deletion.

04. Who can access my file?

Anyone who knows the 6-digit PIN can download your file within the 24-hour window. PINs are randomly generated and not guessable by normal means. Do not share your PIN publicly or with people you don't trust.

05. Do we use cookies or tracking?

No analytics. No advertising cookies. No third-party trackers. The only cookie set is Django's CSRF cookie — a security token required for form submissions. It contains no personal information.

06. Do we share data with third parties?

No. We do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties. Your file exists only on this server and is deleted after 24 hours.

07. What files are NOT allowed?

By using SpiddyWeb, you agree not to upload:

  • ✕ Malware, viruses, or any executable designed to cause harm
  • ✕ Copyrighted material you do not have the right to distribute
  • ✕ Illegal content of any kind
  • ✕ Personal data of others without their consent

Misuse may result in content removal. This is a tool built for honest, quick file sharing — use it responsibly.

08. Data retention

All uploaded files are automatically and permanently deleted 24 hours after upload. We do not archive, backup, or retain files beyond this period.

SpiddyWeb is a free tool. It is provided as-is, without warranties of any kind. Use at your own discretion. — With great power comes great responsibility.
