DMCA Policy

Digital Millennium Copyright Act — Last updated: August 2026

01. Our stance on copyright

SpiddyWeb respects intellectual property rights. We take copyright infringement seriously. All uploaded files auto-delete within 24 hours, but if you believe your copyrighted work has been shared without your permission, you can request immediate removal.

02. How to file a DMCA takedown

To request removal of infringing content, provide the following information:

  • → Your full name and contact information (email, address)
  • → Description of the copyrighted work you believe is being infringed
  • → The 6-digit PIN or URL of the infringing file on SpiddyWeb
  • → A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner
  • → A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, under penalty of perjury
  • → Your physical or electronic signature

03. What happens after a takedown request

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will promptly remove the identified content. Given SpiddyWeb's 24-hour auto-deletion policy, most infringing content will already be gone by the time a notice is processed.

04. Counter-notification

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notification. Counter-notifications must include your contact information, identification of the removed content, a statement under penalty of perjury that removal was a mistake, and your consent to jurisdiction.

05. Abuse of DMCA process

Filing a DMCA takedown notice for content that does not infringe your copyright — or that you do not own — is a legal violation. Misuse of this process may expose you to liability under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

SpiddyWeb automatically deletes all files after 24 hours. Most copyright concerns will resolve themselves within that window. For urgent matters, file a notice immediately.
